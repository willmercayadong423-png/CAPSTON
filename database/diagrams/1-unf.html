WHY THIS FAILS

  • Repeating groups — requests nest inside the student page
  • Non-atomic cells — name, dates, lists in one field
  • No key — nothing uniquely identifies a row
  • Update anomaly — one email fix = many pages
  • Insert / delete anomalies — no-request students can't exist; deleting the last request erases the student
    • ✗ NOT 1NF Next → make every cell atomic, flatten the repeating group, choose a primary key.
